Getting Started

Get your business bookings running on WhatsApp in 4 simple steps.

Create your account

Go to tivoria.site and fill in your business name, phone number, and choose your URL. Takes 30 seconds.

Connect WhatsApp

Go to developers.facebook.com/apps → click Create App → enter App name & App contact email → under Use cases choose Connect with customers through WhatsApp → connect or create a Business portfolio → review Requirements & Overview → click Create App. Then in the left sidebar open Use cases → Connect with customers through WhatsApp → Customize: copy your Phone Number ID from API Setup, generate a permanent Access Token in Business Settings → System Users, and paste the Callback URL & Verify Token under Configuration (subscribe to messages).

Add your services

In Dashboard → Services, add what you offer (e.g. Consultation, 30min, ₹500). You can create categories to group them. Add your team members in the Staff tab.

Go live!

Share your WhatsApp number with customers. When they text "Hi", Tivoria shows your services and lets them book with tappable buttons. You get notified instantly.

Dashboard Guide

Booking trends, revenue by service, staff utilization charts. Understand your business at a glance.

Business profile, booking rules (advance days, cancel policy), notification preferences, WhatsApp connection, operating hours, and integrations.

How Your Customers Book

Your customers don't need to download anything. They just use WhatsApp.

Customer texts "Hi"

Tivoria greets them and shows your services as a tappable list.

Pick a service

Customer taps a service from the list. Tivoria shows available dates.

Pick date & time

Tappable date list, then time slots with staff names. All real-time availability.

Confirm booking

Summary with Confirm/Cancel buttons. If deposit required, payment link sent first.

Reminder & feedback

Auto reminder before appointment. After visit, rating request. High ratings get Google review prompt.

Customer can also:

  • Type cancel to cancel current flow
  • View upcoming bookings and cancel them
  • Type help me to request human assistance

Payments & Deposits

Reduce no-shows by collecting deposits before confirming bookings.

How deposits work

  • Mark a service as "requires deposit" in Dashboard → Services
  • Set deposit amount (e.g. ₹200 for a ₹2000 service)
  • Enable Razorpay in Settings → Integrations
  • When customer books, they get a Razorpay payment link in WhatsApp
  • Booking confirms only after payment

Setting up Razorpay

  • Create account at razorpay.com
  • Get your Key ID and Key Secret from Dashboard → Settings → API Keys
  • Contact Tivoria support to configure webhook

Frequently Asked Questions

Do my customers need to download an app?▼
No. Customers book entirely through WhatsApp, which they already have. No app download, no website visit needed.
What do I need to get started?▼
A WhatsApp Business number with Cloud API access (from Meta), and a Tivoria account. Setup takes about 10 minutes.
How do I get a WhatsApp Business API number?▼
Go to developers.facebook.com, create a Meta Business account, create an app, and add the WhatsApp product. You'll get a Phone Number ID and Access Token. Enter these in your Tivoria dashboard under Settings → WhatsApp Connection.
Can I have multiple team members?▼
Yes! On the Multi-Staff plan (₹999/mo), you can have up to 10 team members. Each member has their own schedule and assigned services. Customers see available slots across all team members.
How does Tivoria prevent double bookings?▼
Tivoria uses 3 layers of protection: real-time slot calculation checks all existing bookings, row-level database locking prevents race conditions, and a database constraint is the final safety net. Double bookings are impossible.
Can I manage my business from WhatsApp only?▼
Yes! Type "today" to see bookings, "stats" for analytics, "add service" to add services, "cancel <id>" to cancel bookings. The web dashboard gives you more power, but WhatsApp commands cover daily operations.
What happens if I pause bookings?▼
Type "pause" and customers will see a message that you're temporarily unavailable. Existing bookings are not affected. Type "resume" when you're ready to accept new bookings.
Do you support multiple languages?▼
Currently English, Hindi, and Tamil message templates are supported. More languages are being added. The booking flow (buttons and lists) works regardless of language.
Can I collect deposits to reduce no-shows?▼
Yes. Mark any service as requiring a deposit, set the amount, and enable Razorpay in Settings. Customers get a payment link in WhatsApp. Booking only confirms after payment.
Is there a free trial?▼
Yes, all plans come with a 7-day free trial. No credit card required to start.
Can I organize services into categories?▼
Yes. Create parent categories and add child services under them. Customers see a clean menu with categories first, then individual services within each.
How do reminders work?▼
Tivoria automatically sends a WhatsApp reminder to customers before their appointment (default: 24 hours before). You can configure the timing in Settings → Notifications.
Can customers leave reviews?▼
After a completed booking, Tivoria asks customers to rate their experience (1-5). If they give 4+ stars and you've set up a Google review URL, they'll be prompted to leave a Google review too.
How do I contact support?▼
Email us at support@tivoria.site. Multi-Staff plan customers get priority support.
